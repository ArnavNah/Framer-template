How are the predictions created?
Our analytical team reviews the fixture schedule every day, evaluating performance data, squad news, and bookmaker odds pricing. Every tip is recorded before match kickoff and automatically graded won, lost, or void upon match completion. You receive them via our Telegram bot @betsmartbot: request them anytime using the command /tips.

No. You do not need to register anywhere or deposit funds into any bookmaker. You receive the predictions regardless of where – or even if – you bet. We never push specific betting operators on you.
