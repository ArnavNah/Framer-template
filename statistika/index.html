A win rate is only as reliable as the integrity of how it is tracked. Here is our exact standard:

Seamless Logging

Every published pick is permanently logged into our database – nothing is ever deleted or edited after the fact.

Timestamped Before Kickoff

Every tip receives a timestamp before the match starts. There are zero retrospective "predictions" here.

Losses Count In Full

Losing bets remain visible and are factored 100% into our win rate and ROI. A record without losses is not a track record.

What "Void" Means

If a match is postponed or cancelled, the bet is settled as void: your stake is refunded and it counts as neither a win nor a loss.
