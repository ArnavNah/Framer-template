4. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Please direct any concerns or complaints to us first – we resolve the vast majority of inquiries directly and quickly.

If an amicable resolution cannot be reached, you may contact the Consumer Disputes Commission at the Consumer Protection and Technical Regulatory Authority (TTJA) or civil courts.

For cross-border disputes within the EU, free advice is available through the European Consumer Centre (ECC). The European Commission's ODR platform discontinued service on July 20, 2025.

For data privacy matters, the relevant supervisory authority is the Data Protection Inspectorate (AKI).

5. Responsible Gambling

Participation in sports betting and gambling is strictly permitted only for individuals aged 18 and older. Gambling can become addictive.

If gambling affects your wellbeing, seek help: 15410.ee – Gambling Addiction Counseling Center helpline 15410 (Mon–Fri 10:00–18:00). Counseling and therapy services are free and confidential.

You can also register on the national self-exclusion list (HAMPI) maintained by the Tax and Customs Board. Licensed bookmakers are legally obligated to verify this registry before each gaming session.
